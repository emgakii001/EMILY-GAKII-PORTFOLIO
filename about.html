Currently building Finance + Data + Technology

I've always been drawn to numbers. My interest in finance started surprisingly early — as a child, I was fascinated by the idea of working with money, managing it well, and eventually earning and managing my own. What started as a simple curiosity grew into a genuine interest in accounting, finance, and the role good financial information plays in running a business.

Today, I help bring structure and clarity to financial and business information. I enjoy taking information that is scattered, complicated, or difficult to use and turning it into something clear, organized, and useful — whether that means reconciling financial records, analyzing business data, building a dashboard, or identifying a pattern that deserves attention.
