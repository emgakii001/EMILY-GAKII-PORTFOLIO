01 — Bookkeeping & Financial Records

Good decisions start with reliable financial records.

Keeping your books up to date isn't just about recording transactions. It's about having financial information you can rely on when you need to understand how your business is doing, track where money is going, or make your next decision.

I help small businesses keep their financial records organized, accurate, and up to date — so you spend less time trying to piece together the numbers and more time focusing on your business.

My Approach

More than recording numbers

I don't see bookkeeping as simply entering transactions and moving on to the next task. I pay attention to the information behind the numbers.

If something doesn't reconcile, looks inconsistent, or needs clarification, I want to understand it and communicate it rather than simply work around it.
