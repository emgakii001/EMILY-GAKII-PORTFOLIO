          06

          Data & Confidentiality Notes

          Confidentiality: Confidential information has been excluded from this portfolio presentation. The dashboard contains only selected financial performance and regulatory indicators suitable for demonstration purposes. Bank account numbers and bank balances were removed at source and have not been reconstructed, inferred, or estimated.

          Data Quality & Reconciliation Checks

          Checks performed before this dashboard was produced.

          • All worksheets in the source workbook inspected
          • Regulatory ratio table and branch profitability table identified as source data
          • May 2025 branch surplus figures sum to KES 39,249,479.95 — reconciles exactly with the workbook total
          • May 2026 branch surplus figures sum to KES 40,899,958.40 — reconciles exactly with the workbook total
          • Absolute annual change recalculated as May 2026 − May 2025 for every branch
          • Annual growth % recalculated directly from source values for every branch
          • Regulatory ratio status cross-checked against the stated MIN / MAX / range standard for each ratio
          • No confidential banking information included
          • Currency displayed throughout in KES with two decimal precision on totals
          Flagged inconsistency (not silently corrected): "Institutional Capital to Total Asset" is recorded in the source workbook as Met in both April and May 2026, with a reported value of 7.8% against a stated minimum standard of 8%. Numerically, 7.8% sits marginally below the 8% minimum. This dashboard reproduces the source-reported status without alteration and flags the discrepancy here for management verification, rather than silently reclassifying it.

          Scope & Limitations

          • This dashboard uses only the Regulatory Ratios and Branch Profitability data contained in the uploaded workbook. No budgets, targets, prior-year regulatory history, or additional financial statements were provided or assumed.
          • "Head Office" is an administrative cost centre, not a revenue-generating branch. It is included in profitability totals (as reported in the source) but excluded from the branch growth ranking narrative, since its year-on-year percentage change (a negative balance becoming more negative) would otherwise read as growth.
          • Where a branch's 2025 base surplus was very small (e.g. Mitunguu), the resulting percentage growth figure is large and is shown alongside the absolute change for context, per Section 8 of the brief.
          • Where a cause for a branch's movement cannot be determined from the data provided, this dashboard states that further investigation is required rather than inferring a reason.
