01 · OVERVIEW

Executive Summary

Network-wide staff cost efficiency for January and February 2026, calculated as total staff cost divided by total income across Head Office and all 15 branches. All ratios below use the SACCO-wide (weighted) method — total cost ÷ total income — not an average of branch percentages.

Network Position at a Glance
Total staff cost, total income and the resulting ratio, January vs February 2026
How to read this dashboard: a lower staff cost-to-income ratio means a smaller share of income is consumed by staff costs; a higher ratio means a greater share is consumed. A high or rising ratio is not automatically a sign of inefficiency — it can also reflect a genuine income decline, a one-off staff cost, or a timing effect. Section 06 (Management Insights) separates what the data shows directly from what requires further investigation.

          Data Quality & Limitations

          This analysis covers January and February 2026 only. It provides a two-month comparison and does not establish a long-term trend; additional monthly data would be required to assess sustained staff-cost efficiency over time.

          Validation performed: for all 32 branch-month records, the sum of the twelve staff-cost components reconciled exactly to the reported Total Staff Cost, and the reported Staff Cost-to-Income % reconciled exactly to Total Staff Cost ÷ Total Income. No missing values, duplicate branch-month records, or material discrepancies were found. Cells recorded as "–" were treated as zero.

          Interpretation caution: a high or rising ratio requires investigation into its underlying cost and income drivers before being labelled a performance problem — it may reflect a genuine income decline, a one-off or accrual-related cost, or a timing effect rather than day-to-day inefficiency. Head Office has a materially different cost/income structure from an operating branch (centralised, non-frontline functions) and comparisons between Head Office and branches should be interpreted cautiously.

          Analysis prepared from the SACCO staff cost & income register for January–February 2026. Figures are in Kenyan Shillings (KES). This dashboard is a portfolio demonstration of financial analysis and management reporting work.
